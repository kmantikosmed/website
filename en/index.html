01 The Doctor

Dr. Mantikos Konstantinos, MD

«General and family medicine sees the person holistically — focusing on prevention, treatment, and lifestyle for their long-term health.»

Dr. Mantikos Konstantinos is a graduate of the Medical School of NKUA (2014) and specialized in General/Family Medicine (2020). With a holistic approach, he combines clinical practice with specialized post-graduate training in Medical Acupuncture, Diabetes, Emergency Pre-hospital Medicine (EKAB), Lifestyle Medicine (University of Thessaly/UTH), and Personal Trainer Nutrition Coach (NKUA), offering comprehensive healthcare services.

  • MD (ΕΚΠΑ) — Specialist General/Family Physician (2020)
  • Post-graduate Training — Diabetes (NKUA), Emergency Medicine (EKAB), Lifestyle Medicine (University of Thessaly/UTH) & Personal Trainer Nutrition Coach (NKUA)
  • Holistic Approach — Certified in Medical Acupuncture, with emphasis on prevention and lifestyle
